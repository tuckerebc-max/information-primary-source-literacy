# Construct and source ledger

**Registry:** `EXP-INFO` / `COMP-INFO-SOURCE`  
**Design status:** `READY_WITH_CONDITIONS`  
**Project snapshot:** 2026-08-11

## Construct boundary

The competency integrates information-need formulation, strategic search, source creation and value, provenance and context, authority/relevance/sufficiency judgment, synthesis, ethical use, and reflection. Primary-source status is contextual; it does not guarantee truth, relevance, completeness, or ethical sufficiency. The ACRL 2026 revision draft is change-detection material, not an adopted replacement.

## Release conditions

Preserve source-native language, exact locators where available, currentness, rights, query/search traces, source-vs-inference labels, original fixtures, accessibility routes, and domain-specific authority limits. Open gates are domain-specific authority adaptations, novice-to-expert evidence, and ethical handling of born-digital or private sources.
